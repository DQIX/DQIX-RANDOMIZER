# DQIX Randomizer 1.8

## How to use it

1. You need your own copy of the **European multilingual** ROM:
   `Dragon Quest IX - Sentinels of the Starry Skies (Europe) (En,Fr,De,Es,It).nds`
   - Serial `YDQP`, MD5 `3a63438fff7db282fa3133e8fd020e85`, 268,435,456 bytes.
   - The application checks the file and refuses any other release. No ROM is
     distributed here.
2. Run `DQIX-Randomizer.exe`. There is nothing to install. The first time, Windows
   SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": the program is simply not
   code-signed. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*, or build it yourself
   from the source.
3. Drop your ROM on the window, pick a seed (or keep the random one), choose a
   preset and click **Randomize**. The randomized ROM, its log and its patch
   are written together in a `DQIX-Randomizer-seed<seed>` folder next to your
   ROM.
4. Start a **new game** in your emulator (melonDS, or DeSmuME with the Dynamic
   Recompiler turned off).

The ROM never leaves your computer: the application works offline and sends
nothing anywhere. The same seed with the same options always gives exactly the
same game, so a seed and the list of options are all you need to share a run.

## What changes

- **A secret boss.** Every ROM now hides a boss of its own, with a short
  quest to reach it, a fight unlike any other in the game, its own theme,
  and a unique reward. Nothing to tick: it is in every ROM, whatever the
  options, and none of the randomizer's draws ever uses its skills, its
  species or its weapon. Finding it is up to you.

- **Bosses keep their escorts, now from the whole bestiary.** With bosses
  randomized, every ordinary monster of a scripted fight (the two bad
  karmours around Hootingham-Gore, the stenchurions, the scarlet fevers...)
  becomes a monster from the bestiary, all different within a fight. A fight
  never gets heavier than the game's fight memory holds (150 kB of models as
  the game declares them, each model counted once, with the monsters a boss
  calls as reinforcements): beyond that, the game silently drops the later
  monsters, which is why some players saw Hootingham-Gore "replaced by a
  monster". The option *monsters around bosses: as before*
  keeps the 1.7 behaviour.
- **Equal 1-to-1 per area.** Every species of the bestiary now gets the same
  number of places per game, big models included (3.4 to 4 areas each over
  200 seeds, against 0.4 to 15 before), within the same memory budget per
  area as the original game. No option: this is the default.
- **The ranger's last spell is randomized.** Moreheal at level 34 was never
  read by the randomizer and stayed in place; it is now drawn like the
  others, and listed in *Create your ROM*. The ranger's skill tree
  (Ruggedness) also shows its name there.
- **The three rare shops are at 200 %** instead of 800 %. The fixed prices
  (skill books, seeds, 5-star items) keep their rules; the silver shield
  still shows about 30,500.

- **Mortamor's three fights show all three bosses.** They carry three bosses
  each; with bosses randomized, three big ones overflowed the fight's memory
  and only the first showed up. Heavy picks are now swapped with light ones
  from other fights (every boss still comes out), and with *a new boss at
  every fight* these three fights keep the boss drawn when the ROM is built.
- **Wi-Fi quest bosses are a family of their own** (Moby Pick II, Yore,
  Rover, King Godfrey, Tyrannosaura Wrecks, Nodoph): *equal boss families*
  now gives a quarter to each of story, Wi-Fi quests, grottos and legacy
  bosses, and they have their own *from* setting.
- **A family can stop.** Next to *from*, each boss family has a *no more
  from* setting: for instance, no more story or Wi-Fi bosses from Corvus on,
  only legacy and grotto bosses.
- **Random boss spells and abilities** (Bosses tab, off by default): on its
  own, bosses draw new spells and abilities at every fight and keep their
  plain attack; with the monsters' spells or abilities ticked, bosses
  follow the same mode. The two cutscene fights keep theirs.
- **Fixed kits show every action again.** With *one fixed kit per
  monster*, some actions ran with no text and no animation, just a camera
  move: Psyche Up on the monsters that have it, the poison tick, the
  "… returns to normal" messages, and the monsters' tension that climbs
  three steps. The game only reads the first 8 actions of a presentation
  list, and the randomizer made them up to 32 long.
- **New options, all off by default:** *rare books* (sparkly spots give a
  book once in a hundred pickups instead of about one in five), *generous
  milestones* (Cap'n Max Meddlin' has a milestone at every 1 to 10 mini
  medals, each giving a 3 to 5-star item), *recipes in bookshelves* (each
  recipe book teaches other recipes, never the same one twice in the game,
  and names the recipes it teaches), *chest ambushes: any monster* (random
  spawn mode: a trapped chest releases any monster, drawn like a field
  spawn, and about one blue or grotto chest in twenty hides one).
- **Trapped chests always fight back.** In random spawn mode, when the
  chest's own monster (cannibox...) cannot appear where you are, another
  monster is drawn in its place instead of nothing.

## Known issues

- Tested on emulators only.
- A savestate made on a 1.7 ROM does not load on a 1.8 ROM (the file layout changed): keep playing from your in-game save.
- Recipe bookshelves: the game remembers each book read, not the recipes.
  On a save where a book was already read, its shelf teaches nothing new;
  start a new game to get the randomized recipes from every shelf.
