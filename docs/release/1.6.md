# DQIX Randomizer 1.6

## How to use it

1. You need your own copy of the **European multilingual** ROM:
   `Dragon Quest IX - Sentinels of the Starry Skies (Europe) (En,Fr,De,Es,It).nds`
   - Serial `YDQP`, MD5 `3a63438fff7db282fa3133e8fd020e85`, 268,435,456 bytes.
   - The application checks the file and refuses any other release. No ROM is
     distributed here.
2. Run `DQIX-Randomizer.exe`. There is nothing to install. The first time, Windows
   SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": the program is simply not
   code-signed. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*, or build it yourself
   from the source.
3. Drop your ROM on the window, pick a seed (or keep the random one), choose a
   preset and click **Randomize**. After about 25 seconds, a playable
   `DQIX-Randomizer-seed<seed>.nds` appears next to your ROM.
4. Start a **new game** in your emulator (melonDS, or DeSmuME with the Dynamic
   Recompiler turned off).

The ROM never leaves your computer: the application works offline and sends
nothing anywhere. The same seed with the same options always gives exactly the
same game, so a seed and the list of options are all you need to share a run.

The window is available in **English, French, German, Spanish and Italian**;
switch at any time from the menu at the top right.

## What's new since 1.5

- **Monster spells, new at every battle** (option). In every battle, a
  monster's spells are drawn at random among the 53 battle spells. Healing
  and support spells are only cast when they are useful, as in the original
  game, and a monster gets the MP its new spells need. A second checkbox also
  gives one spell to monsters that had none.
- **Monster abilities, new at every battle, with their real animation**
  (option). In every battle, a monster's abilities are drawn among 256
  abilities of monsters and heroes: a slime can breathe fire, throw a
  boomerang or use Gigagash. Each ability is shown with its own animation,
  taken from the monster or hero who has it; about ten of them are available
  in each battle, all 256 over a game. A second checkbox also gives one
  ability to monsters that had none. Bosses keep their own actions.
- **Monsters fight in groups again.** A randomized field monster now comes
  with companions, as in the original game.
- **The Whistle works.** It calls a monster and starts a battle, and you no
  longer walk through monsters afterwards.
- **Three ways to randomize field monsters**, as in the Universal Pokémon
  Randomizer:
  - *random at every spawn* (default): any monster, every time one appears;
  - *1-to-1 per area*: in each area, a monster is always replaced by the same
    other one;
  - *1-to-1 for the whole game*: a monster becomes the same other one
    everywhere.
  The two 1-to-1 modes patch no game code: more stable, less varied.
- **Boss fights, four new options:**
  - *equal boss families*: story, grotto and legacy bosses come out as often
    as each other (without it, legacy bosses, which exist in three levels,
    take half of the fights);
  - *story bosses only*: only story bosses are shuffled; grotto and legacy
    fights stay as in the game;
  - *boss or monster*: a boss fight can also be against an ordinary monster;
  - *new boss at every attempt*: another boss is drawn each time you enter
    the fight, for example after a defeat (random mode).
- **Bosses on the field** (experimental) is now a checkbox of the
  application, off by default and in no preset.
- **Gold amounts** in containers are redrawn: 1 to 10,000 G in chests, 1 to
  1,000 G in pots, barrels and cupboards.
- **Techniques can keep their weapon** (option): a weapon technique moved to
  another skill tree still needs the weapon it was made for.
- The application is organised in **four tabs** (Monsters, Bosses, Items and
  shops, Party); a sub-option greys out when it would change nothing.
- **README** rewritten.

## Fixes

- Aquila and Corvus from two cutscene fights (9999 HP, every stat at 255)
  could replace another boss with `--boss`, making that fight unwinnable.
  They are no longer drawn, and their two fights are left as in the game.

- The Whistle summoned nothing with randomized monsters, and the party then
  walked through every monster (ZER-68).
- Sterling's whistle and the five Dragon warrior pieces, which the story
  gives, could be found in containers, drops and shops; they are now out of
  every draw (ZER-66, ZER-72).
- Battles against randomized field monsters had a single monster (ZER-67).

## Known issues

- A monster that calls for help can only be joined by monsters already in the
  battle (a random reinforcement is planned).

- Trapped chests still hold only cannibox, mimic and pandora's box: an option
  to put any monster in them did not work in game and was withdrawn (ZER-21).
