# DQIX Randomizer 1.7

## How to use it

1. You need your own copy of the **European multilingual** ROM:
   `Dragon Quest IX - Sentinels of the Starry Skies (Europe) (En,Fr,De,Es,It).nds`
   - Serial `YDQP`, MD5 `3a63438fff7db282fa3133e8fd020e85`, 268,435,456 bytes.
   - The application checks the file and refuses any other release. No ROM is
     distributed here.
2. Run `DQIX-Randomizer.exe`. There is nothing to install. The first time, Windows
   SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": the program is simply not
   code-signed. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*, or build it yourself
   from the source.
3. Drop your ROM on the window, pick a seed (or keep the random one), choose a
   preset and click **Randomize**. The randomized ROM, its log and its patch
   are written together in a `DQIX-Randomizer-seed<seed>` folder next to your
   ROM.
4. Start a **new game** in your emulator (melonDS, or DeSmuME with the Dynamic
   Recompiler turned off).

The ROM never leaves your computer: the application works offline and sends
nothing anywhere. The same seed with the same options always gives exactly the
same game, so a seed and the list of options are all you need to share a run.

## Update of October 20: monster stats, sparkly spots, change a ROM mid-game

The application on this page was replaced by a new one. Both presets now
include sparkly spots; apart from that, seeds made with the first 1.7
application give the same game, and the other new options are off unless you
tick them.

- **Monster stats.** HP, MP, attack, defence, agility and resistances (7
  elements, 14 ailments); the bosses have their own option in the Bosses
  tab, with the same choices. Two ranges: ×0.5 to ×2 of the
  original value, resistances one step up or down; or *chaos*, anything over
  the whole range of the game. Drawn once when the ROM is built, or **again
  in every battle**. The prologue's tutorial fight is never changed. In
  **Create your ROM**, each monster keeps its values, gets fixed values, or
  draws them (at build time or in every battle) between bounds you set.
- **Sparkly spots** give any regular item, never equipment, never a quest
  item. Each map has a pool of up to 127 items; **16 are drawn every time
  you enter the map**, then one at each pickup, so a spot rarely gives the
  same thing twice. All 143 items are somewhere. Options: items close to the
  original rank, or one fixed item per spot. In **Create your ROM**, you pick
  each map's pool, or each spot's item. In both presets.
- **A fixed kit for each monster.** With monster spells or abilities ticked,
  *a fixed kit for each monster* draws them once, when the ROM is built:
  every slime has the same spells and abilities for the whole game, and
  each ability keeps its real animation (copied into the monster's own
  battle model).
- **Change a randomized ROM during a game.** Drop the ROM of your game on
  the window (or *Change a randomized ROM...*): it ticks its seed and
  options again; change what you want, and
  the new ROM replaces the old one under the same name, so your save keeps
  working.
- Monster spells and abilities are **no longer experimental**.
- **Create your ROM**: a search field finds a line in the long lists (438
  monsters).

## What's new since 1.6

### Create your own ROM

A fifth tab, **Create your ROM**, where you decide every case yourself — for
challenge runs, races or just fun. Start from the original game, from your
options of the other tabs or from a file, re-roll a single category, change
any case by hand (a changed case is pinned and survives the next re-roll),
and save everything in a file to share. The same file always gives the same
ROM.

What you can set, case by case:

- **Bosses**: which boss each scripted fight brings (story, grottos, legacy,
  other fights), fixed or drawn again at every visit.
- **Shops**: what every shop sells.
- **Chests, pots, barrels and grottos**: blue chests, red chests, the lines
  of the containers' item lists, grotto chests.
- **Alchemy**: the ingredients of every recipe.
- **Cap'n Max Meddlin'**: his ten milestones and his medal shop.
- **Skill trees**, **vocation spells** and **vocations** (which ones are open,
  the starting one).
- **Field monsters**: random at every spawn, one monster for another per
  area, or for the whole game — and you pick each replacement.
- **Battle drops**: the list of items monsters drop at every battle.
- **Monster spells and abilities**: the six actions of every monster; each
  slot fixed, a spell drawn at every battle or an ability drawn at every
  battle; and the shared pool they are drawn from.

Anything goes there, even unbalanced choices (4-5 star items in any shop).
Only what would make the game unfinishable is locked (quest items, the
prologue fight, the thief's key, the skill-book techniques, the bosses'
own actions), and the locks can be lifted with one checkbox. The main button
follows the tab you are on: from this tab it builds your custom ROM (the
options of the other tabs do not count), from the other tabs it uses their
options. On the command
line: `--exporter-config` writes the file of a ROM you build, `--config`
applies one.

### Monsters and bosses

- **Battle music follows the boss.** A boss fight plays the music of the
  boss you get, not the one of the fight's original boss.
- **Bosses on the field chase you.** With *bosses on the field*
  (experimental), the bosses now move and chase like ordinary monsters.
  Only 14 bosses can appear on the field.
- **Legacy bosses at their first level only.** Each legacy boss exists at
  three levels, so it used to come out three times as often as a story boss.
  Now only level 1 is drawn; *all three legacy boss levels* brings back the
  1.6 behaviour.
- **Choose when grotto and legacy bosses arrive.** Two lists in the Bosses
  tab: from which story boss on a story fight can get a grotto boss, and
  (separately) a legacy boss. Default: from the start, as in 1.6.

### Items

- **Grotto chests are randomized.** Their items come from the whole pool,
  like blue chests, with the same odds of item, gold and ambush per rank.
- **Rare items everywhere, by default**, in both presets. Untick *rare items
  everywhere* to get the 1.6 behaviour, where rarity follows the rank.
- **A plain item draw by default.** Chests no longer reserve a share for
  consumables. Tick *balance equipment and consumables* for about a third of
  herbs, potions and seeds, as in 1.6.
- **Alchemy ingredients**, in both presets. Every Krak Pot recipe still makes
  the same item, in the same quantities, but asks for other ingredients. Both
  recipes of a great-success pair keep the same ingredients.
- **Cap'n Max Meddlin's rewards**, in both presets. Mini medals are as rare
  as any other item once the loot is randomized, so every count is divided by
  4; *original mini medal counts* keeps them.
- **Quest items stay in their shops.** The flowing dress, the watermaul wand
  and the silver shield, needed for a post-game quest, are always sold where
  the original game sells them, in every mode.
- **The bestiary shows the drops of your ROM**, not the original ones (when
  drops are drawn at every battle, it shows the original list).

### Application

- **One folder per seed** next to your ROM.
- **Tabs easier to see** (bold, larger).
- The three options that need *random at every spawn* say so.

## Fixes

- **Wrong colours on the field** (monsters painted on the hero's or the
  text's palette after a while): fixed (ZER-40).
- **Field monster modes *1-to-1 per area* and *1-to-1 for the whole game***
  measured the model size of the wrong monster, so a map's model budget was
  wrong (present in 1.6). No visible effect was found in game, but the
  budget is now right.
- The bestiary showed the original drops since 1.2.

## Known issues

- *Bosses on the field* is experimental: only 14 bosses, and some may
  misbehave.
- In **Create your ROM**: pins set on the lines of the containers' item lists
  are dropped by a re-roll (the list is replaced); a very scattered battle
  drop list may not fit (the application says so).
